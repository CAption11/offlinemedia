================================================================================
PROJECT STRUCTURE DECISION
================================================================================

OfflineMedia is divided into two operational sections:

1. MAINLOAD
   Downloadable/local Windows application.

2. PORTABLE
   Google Colab/GPU development and real-generation environment.

They share the generation core. Portable is the laboratory used to prove real
AI generation before Mainload presents that generation as an end-user feature.
