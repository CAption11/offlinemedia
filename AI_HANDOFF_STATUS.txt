FIX 1 - a still image passed as a generated video.
validate_output required a stream with codec_type == video. ffprobe models a
still image as exactly that: a one-frame video stream. A lone PNG therefore
satisfied the check and the notebook would print "REAL VIDEO GENERATION ...
PASSED" for it. Now rejected, but only when ffprobe positively reports one
frame AND no duration - animated WEBP often reports nb_frames as "N/A" and must
not be failed. Verified: the new test does not raise against the old validator.

FIX 2 - validation was duplicated in notebook cell 8, same weakness included.
The notebook now imports validate_output from scripts/test_generation.py, so
the CLI and notebook cannot drift and both carry the fix.

ANSWER TO REVIEW QUESTION 4 - the trigger lifecycle gap is real.
Nothing moved a job past "running", so an in-flight job and a finished one were
indistinguishable, and a crashed runtime looked like a working one. Added
complete_trigger(job_id, status="completed"|"failed"): re-reads for a current
SHA, REFUSES to overwrite a different job than the one claimed, stamps
finished_at, takes an optional detail. Wired into the notebook on the
generation-failure and validation-failure paths as well as the happy path.

Tests: new tests/test_output_validation.py (9). test_trigger_listener.py now
